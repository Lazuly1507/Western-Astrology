# Vietnamese astrology content and SEO

1. Follow the active PRD: zodiac discovery/details and knowledge library are MVP; personalized daily horoscopes and paid reports are not.
2. Maintain a glossary separating signs, constellations, planets, houses and aspects. Date ranges are approximate editorial guides, not a Sun-sign calculation for boundary births.
3. Provide a beginner summary, optional advanced explanation, inspected sources, reviewer/update metadata and honest limits of symbolic interpretation.
4. Write original Vietnamese content; inspect licenses before reusing text/images. Do not copy competitor readings or imply unverified author credentials.
5. Specify SSR/SSG, titles, canonical, sitemap/internal links and truthful structured data for public pages. Exclude private birth/chart/chat/account/admin data from metadata, search and shared caches; noindex does not replace auth.
6. Review headings, link purpose, image alternatives, diacritics and content freshness. No ranking/traffic/conversion claim without measurements.

Acceptance: twelve distinct sign concepts without deterministic stereotypes; public/private SEO boundary enforced; factual citations support claims; no outside-MVP personalized content.
