# Grounded constellation brand experience

1. Read `docs/product/brand-direction.md` and `docs/design/constellation-experience.md`. Brand names and visual concepts remain proposals pending approval.
2. Keep three layers coherent: approachable signs/Big 3, technical natal data, and a symbolic constellation narrative. Never present symbolic fate language as scientifically validated or certain prediction.
3. Constellation nodes must map to existing chart body/angle IDs; edges must map to calculated aspect IDs. Preserve the same chart revision and selection across standard and constellation views. No random placement, invented aspect or fake locked node to create a more impressive story.
4. Define an original palette, line/glyph system, typography and motion grammar. Do not copy proprietary game artwork, icons, fonts, character constellations, layouts, names or progression trees. Keep an asset provenance record; do not imply an IP clearance guarantee.
5. Separate expressive brand geometry from astronomical sky coordinates. Explain any projection and maintain a technical chart and semantic text alternative. Unknown birth time cannot acquire missing ASC/houses through narrative decoration.
6. AI can change tone between plain and mystical, but fact/rule evidence and uncertainty must remain identical. Do not introduce certainty, health/financial claims, gamified purchases or outside-MVP forecasting.

Acceptance: every meaningful node/edge has evidence, original assets have provenance, reduced-motion/touch/keyboard paths work, and the standard chart remains authoritative.
